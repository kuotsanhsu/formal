# Gödel Ideas And Questions

This file tracks side questions, recurring intuitions, and tangents worth revisiting. It should not collect every passing thought.

## Parallel Topic: Attention

Status: serious parallel topic, subordinate to the main Gödel project.

Initial thesis to investigate:

- Memory may be understood as an attention mechanism.
- Forgetting is not merely failure; it can be a feature that makes focus possible.
- Remembering or knowing everything may collapse into something functionally equivalent to remembering or knowing nothing, because selection and salience disappear.
- Focus develops over time partly because memory is selective.

Research directions to pursue later:

- Philosophers of attention, memory, salience, and forgetting.
- Psychoanalytic accounts of forgetting, repression, attention, and psychic economy.
- Buddhist treatments of attention, mindfulness, memory, attachment, and non-distraction.
- Connections to formal systems: what must be selected, encoded, ignored, or abstracted for a proof to become intelligible.

Candidate names and traditions to check later:

- William James on attention.
- Husserl and phenomenology of inner time-consciousness.
- Bergson on memory.
- Freud and later psychoanalytic work on forgetting and repression.
- Simone Weil on attention.
- Buddhist Abhidharma and mindfulness traditions.
